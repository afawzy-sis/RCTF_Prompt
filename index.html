Bad Prompt vs. Professional Prompt

BAD PROMPT

"Create a lesson plan about Present Simple."

What is missing?

  • Grade & Age?
  • Level & Duration?
  • Learning Objectives?
  • Activities & Assessment?

PROFESSIONAL

Act as an experienced English teacher.

Create a 45-min lesson plan for Grade 6 (Age 11-12, A1-A2).
Topic: Present Simple – Daily Routines.

Include: Starter, Presentation, Guided Practice, Assessment.

Format: Use student-centered activities. Present in a table.

4. COMPLETE

MASTER LESSON PLAN PROMPT الأمر الشامل لتخطيط الدروس

[ROLE] Act as an experienced instructional designer and educational technology specialist.
[TASK] Your task is to help me transform the uploaded teaching material into a structured lesson plan.
[ANALYZE] First, analyze the uploaded material before generating anything. Identify the topic, learning objectives, key concepts, skills, activities, resources, and assessment opportunities.
Then analyze the uploaded lesson-plan template and identify its structure, required sections, sequence, and formatting logic. Do not redesign or modify the template.
[LIMITS] Create the lesson plan using the same structure and sequence as the uploaded template. Use only information supported by the uploaded teaching material. Do not invent missing curriculum content. If essential information is missing, clearly identify it and ask for clarification.
[ADAPT & INCLUDE] Adapt the language, examples, activities, and level to the learners described in the source material. Include appropriate:
  • Learning objectives
  • Timing
  • Teaching & learning activities
  • Differentiation
  • Formative assessment
  • Resources
  • Student-centered learning strategies
[FORMAT & OUTPUT] Before finalizing, review the lesson plan against the original material and template and identify any inconsistencies. Output the final lesson plan in the uploaded template structure.

My Lesson Planning Workflow

  • 01 DEFINE: What do I want?
  • 02 CONTEXT: Who are my learners?
  • 03 PROVIDE: What are the resources/templates?
  • 04 INSTRUCT: What exactly should it do?
  • 05 CONSTRAIN: What are the limits?
  • 06 REVIEW: Does the output make educational sense?
  • 07 REFINE: What needs adjustment?
